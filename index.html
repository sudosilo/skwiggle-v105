SKWIGGLE

You lay a line and you cannot stop. Faster thins it, slower fattens it, and it stays forever. PLAY: SURVIVAL (crossing your line ends you) and RACE (barrels against three rivals, with pickups on the course). DRAW: PAINT (crossing cycles through fourteen inks), WASH (translucent ink, overlaps deepen), OUTLINE (decorate a silhouette), CHAOS (three lines at once, one yours), ANTI CHAOS (two curators bring order to your noise). A lone curator can also be summoned onto any PAINT, WASH, OUTLINE, or AUTO paper, with its own vehicle pick independent of yours, and pausing paint/wash/outline brings up a paint can to bucket-fill a spot, blank or already painted, with a color you choose. HIDE UI clears the d-pad and strip off the screen so you can reach or admire every corner of the canvas; tap it again to bring them back. Tapping MENU never erases anything: a RESUME button brings you straight back to exactly where you left off. WATCH: AUTO (the line draws itself through shape programs) and VIZ (twelve pixels dance your microphone or a song across ten effects). PAUSED, PAINT/WASH/OUTLINE also offer SAVE ART (a PNG of the piece, plus a signed JSON of every real choice made to build it, in order) and MINT (that same piece minted as an NFT on Base, to whichever wallet is connected when you tap it, for a small mint fee plus gas). MINT needs a wallet-capable browser, Brave's built-in wallet works directly, or open the page from inside a wallet app's own browser tab; plain Chrome or Firefox on a phone has nothing to connect to.
